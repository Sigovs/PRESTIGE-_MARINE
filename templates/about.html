Developed out of the passion Brett David, CEO of Prestige Imports, had for everything marine and particularly his love for yachting in Miami, he recognized a connection between his clients that owned premium exotic automobiles and the luxury lifestyle of the yachting industry from which Prestige Marine was born.

Prestige Marine, a division of Prestige Imports, is not your typical marine dealership. At Prestige Marine, we bring the same passion and drive to the marine industry that has made Prestige Imports so successful in the automobile industry since 1977.

Representing the most iconic yacht brands on Miami’s waterways, such as VanDutch Yachts, Pardo Yachts, Iguana Yachts, Tecnomar for Lamborghini 63 Yacht, Midnight Express, and SEABOB the world’s fastest underwater scooter, the Prestige “client first” philosophy is practiced by Land, Air & now Sea.

Prestige Marine is dedicated to providing a different kind of marine experience where we are not just selling yachts we are providing lifestyles and pride ourselves on exceeding the expectations of our customers.

With our tremendous recourses and relationships in the yachting industry, we are able to offer clients unparalleled service in building your dream yacht, brokerage services, yacht management, luxury charter, and a dedicated aftersales service center.
