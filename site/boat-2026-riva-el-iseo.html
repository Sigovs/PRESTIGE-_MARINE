About this boat

Riva El-Iseo is the brands first full electric powerboat that launched the new E-Luxury segment under the banner of subtainability. A remarkable blend of innovation and heritage, this runabout with elegant, refined lines features the "Azzuro California" blue hull, a super cool color. This electric vessel is unmistakably Riva quality and sophistication.

Three speed modes known as Top, Classic and Eco allowing a top speed performance of 40 knots and a cruising speed of 25 knots providing up to 50 nautical miles of range. In addition there is maximum comfort with a u-shaped sofa, hi-lo table, bimini top, glass windshield, under seat storage and drawer cockpit refrigerator
