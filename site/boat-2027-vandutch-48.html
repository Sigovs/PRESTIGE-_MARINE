About this boat

New VanDutch 48 complete at the factory and ready for delivery to the U.S.

VanDutch 48 Yacht represents the long awaited crossover that fills the vacuum between the VanDutch 40 and 55 models. Head turning with a distinctive tender profile, including the unmistakable vertical bow and minimalistic deck lines, it provides enhanced volume and interior capacity from its 48-feet length. Besides her sizeable, convertible cabin, the open top deck layout, the large aft and sun pad with hydraulic swim platform the VanDutch 48 offers great yacht performances with top speeds up to 36 knots thanks to her twin Volvo 725hp in a traditional line shaft propulsion.

This great VanDutch day cruiser, will entertain you with the yachts Illuminate lighting system, 2 x 12" Helm Screens, joystick thruster controls and music remote for ultimate vessel control. Options include the big engine package, Hydraulic swim platform and full length Superyacht Sunshade. The Van Dutch 48 yacht will undoubtedly be the most impressive day boat available on the market!
