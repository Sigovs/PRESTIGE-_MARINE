About this boat

The 52’ Vitesse feels unlike anything else on the water, with open sea capabilities to take you where you want to go. Midnight Express infusion construction paired with select Carbon Fiber sections, make the 52’ Vitesse both agile and nimble but rugged and refined to create a ride unparalleled in the industry. This custom built 52 Vitesse is built with every conceivable option including the first SeaKeeper 10 (designed for much larger yachts) installed on a Midnight Express.

The Company offers the details of this vessel in good faith but cannot guarantee or warrant the accuracy of this information nor warrant the condition of the vessel. A buyer should instruct his agents, or his surveyors, to investigate such details as the buyer desires validated. This vessel is offered subject to prior sale, price change, or withdrawal without notice.
